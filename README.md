A two-line explanation of an everyday thing for the make.fun bounty:
line one is an analogy, line two says what the analogy maps and what
it leaves out.

> A microwave is a tiny lightning box that shakes the water inside your food until it gets hot.
> It maps how invisible waves heat food from the inside, but leaves out that the waves are radio waves, not lightning, and they heat unevenly, so the middle can stay cold.

The entry is exactly two lines and 1–400 characters in total, as the
bounty requires. `main.py` validates the text (line count, total
length, non-empty lines, one sentence per line) before writing and
verifies it again by reading the output file back.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the two lines to `/output/entry.txt`.
